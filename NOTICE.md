# Notice

Tack was inspired by **Tendedero**, a macOS app by Alejandro Buján
(<https://github.com/alejandrobujan/tendedero>) that keeps recent screenshots
within reach at the top of the screen. Thanks to its author for the idea.

Tack is its own work: a Windows app written in Rust and JavaScript, with its
own corkboard design, code, wording, name and icon. It does not use the
Tendedero name or icon, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by
Tendedero's author.

No Tendedero source code or text is copied into Tack. Early Tack development
did study Tendedero's MIT-licensed source, and a few behavioural parameters
match it (for example a 450 ms long press, gusts every 7 to 16 seconds, a
60 ms stagger when clearing, a 1.2 s "Copied" label). Out of caution,
Tendedero's license notice is reproduced below.
